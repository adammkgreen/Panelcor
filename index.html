Supply Chain Balancing

How Panelcor levels production peaks and valleys

In the panel assembly industry, capacity is notoriously volatile. One month a workshop has a 4-week backlog, and the next month technician workbenches sit idle waiting for engineering approvals.

Panelcor balances this dynamic: we route excess buyer demand to panel builders with spare workbench hours, stabilizing shop floor revenues and keeping client projects on schedule.

Architecture & Flow

A systematic marketplace for custom manufacturing.

Learn how Panelcor ensures specification fidelity, competitive quotes, and strict execution from start to finish.

STAGE 01

Submit an RFQ

Buyers upload structured project parameters: CAD drawings, single-line diagrams (SLD), functional circuit schematics, preferred brand lists (e.g., Schneider Electric, Siemens, ABB, Phoenix Contact, Rittal), and specific project delivery deadlines.

Confidentiality First: Engineering files are never indexed publicly. They are stored in isolated vaults and only accessible to verified workshops that sign project-specific non-disclosure agreements.

TYPICAL RFQ ARTIFACTS
Circuit Schematics:EPLAN P8 / PDF
Bill of Materials:Standardized Excel / CSV
Enclosure Envelope:Height, Width, Depth, IP Rating
Testing Protocols:FAT Checklist / Dielectric Test
STAGE 02

Compare Quotations

Instead of unformatted email estimates, Panelcor delivers standardized, itemized quotes. You receive direct comparisons of:

  • Component procurement cost & lead times
  • Mechanical assembly and wire-routing labor hours
  • Documentation, testing & FAT sign-off costs
  • Guaranteed ex-works or delivered shipping date

Transparent Comparison Metric

Quotes reflect real-world capacity. A shop with immediate capacity will offer accelerated turnaround without emergency overtime premiums.

Builder A: 5 Weeks | Stocked Enclosure | IEC Compliant
Builder B: 3.5 Weeks | Full Custom Wiring | Express Bay
STAGE 03

Award the Order

Once the optimal quote is chosen, the order is locked. Both parties agree on milestones: engineering sign-off, component acquisition, assembly start, FAT testing date, and handover.

Panelcor provides contractual framework consistency, ensuring both the manufacturing shop and the customer have clear terms of payment and inspection.

ORDER MILESTONES
Milestone 1:Engineering Approval & BOM Freeze
Milestone 2:Enclosure & Core Hardware In-House
Milestone 3:Wiring & Point-to-Point Verification
Milestone 4:FAT Sign-off & Freight Release
STAGE 04

Track Production

Buyers maintain visibility into panel progress through milestone updates and testing sign-offs. Final routine testing protocols (IEC 61439 clause 11 routine verification) are completed and signed before dispatch.

No surprise delays—if a component delay occurs, it is communicated transparently within the tracking timeline.

Quality Verification Checklist

Standard test reports provided with every completed panel unit:

  • Dielectric and insulation resistance tests
  • Protective conductor continuity tests
  • Visual mechanical torque verification
  • Point-to-point electrical function verification
Company Background

Building resilience into Nordic industrial electrification.

Panelcor was conceived to modernize the physical backbone of the energy transition and industrial automation: the electrical control panel.

The Nordic Electrification Challenge

The Nordic region is leading the world in green industry transitions—from battery manufacturing and fossil-free steel to heat pumps, hydrogen plants, and automated logistics centers. Every single one of these installations requires custom electrical distribution panels, Motor Control Centers (MCCs), and PLC enclosures.

Yet panel manufacturing remains fragmented. Talented panel shops with high technical mastery frequently experience irregular demand cycles. At the same time, machine builders face delivery lead times stretching 20+ weeks simply because their usual supplier is overbooked.

Our Marketplace Mission

Panelcor builds the digital coordination layer that links this distributed capacity. By standardizing the RFQ process and connecting transparent capacity metrics across Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland, we help workshops stay continuously productive while shortening industrial equipment lead times.

Future Roadmap: Full Digital Integration

As the marketplace expands, Panelcor is architected to introduce secure authenticated portals, unified CAD and EPLAN cloud viewers, standardized component library pricing feeds, and automated FAT documentation compliance vaults.
